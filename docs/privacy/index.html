Privacy Policy

Burmese Calendar (မြန်မာပြက္ခဒိန်) · IT Garden Malaysia (RN) · Effective 27 November 2026

Summary

Burmese Calendar does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your language choice and whether Sabbath reminders are on are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you turn on Sabbath reminders, the app asks your permission and schedules local reminders on your device. They are created by the app itself. No push server is used, and nothing is sent over the internet.

Calendar data

All Myanmar dates are calculated on your device using the open-source mmcal algorithm by Dr. Yan Naing Aye. Official public holidays are built into the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/RichardNaing/Burmese-Family-Calendar-.
