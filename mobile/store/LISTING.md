## Description (English)

Burmese Calendar puts the Myanmar calendar on your phone, in Burmese and English, on a tactile paper board: drag or flick the ball into a day to see its Myanmar date.

• Every day shows its Myanmar date: year, month, waxing or waning day, in Burmese numerals
• Full moon and new moon days at a glance
• Sabbath (ဥပုသ်) days marked, with an optional reminder the evening before
• 2027 public holidays from the Union Government's State Gazette announcement
• Thingyan and Myanmar New Year
• Yatyaza, Pyathada, Naga head direction and Mahabote for every day
• Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Myanmar dates are calculated with the open-source mmcal algorithm by Dr. Yan Naing Aye.

Design by IT Garden Malaysia (RN).
